# People review

Maintain source-backed profiles and {agent_name}'s own revisable impressions of the people in {human_name}'s life, {human_name} first.

## Procedure
<!-- personal -->
Run `brain portrait coverage --json`; for each account in `unreadBackfill`, load `{prefix}seed` and run it scoped to that account before the rest of this pass.
<!-- /personal -->

1. Read yesterday's digest in `Conversations/Digests/`. Every user turn labelled with something other than {human_name}'s name is another person speaking: a platform handle or id, or a display name. A handle can be renamed; the stable id on the profile's line for that platform is what identifies them. List the distinct speakers, with what each said and asked and how they responded to the agent.
2. Resolve each speaker to a profile under `{people_dir}/` by the platform id recorded on the profile; for a Demiurge id no profile carries, run `brain people who <id>` and, when listed, create the profile from the template in the canonical folder for the directory name (`{people_dir}/<Name>/Profile.md`, or `{agents_dir}/<Name>/Profile.md` for an intellect), with its id line, never a handle-named stub. A speaker with no matching profile or directory entry is an unknown person: create `{people_dir}/<display name or id>/Profile.md` from the template with the id as its only confirmed identity, the date, and what they said, marked "identity unconfirmed by {human_name}" (the engine treats such a profile as a stranger until {human_name} confirms who it is; then remove the mark), and add an `ask` to CURRENT's `## Needs you`: "a new person messaged me; who is this?" Never merge an unknown id into an existing profile on a name alone.
3. For each person spoken with, write the day's dated entry to `{people_dir}/<Name>/History.md` (newest first: date, channel, what they said and asked, how it went, filed as their claims); a thing coming up for them that they named (a trip, a visit, a result, a date) is written with its date spelled out (`November 2`), so the Upcoming scan carries it into context the week before and update `Profile.md` in place: what they told the agent, filed as their claim with the date; what they asked for and how it was answered (the access log has each request); and the impression. Impressions are the agent's own and say so: how they talk, what they care about, what they expect from the agent, with confidence and counterevidence. Revise an earlier impression when yesterday contradicted it; do not just append. A Profile stays under 300 words and holds only what is stable: identifiers, relationship, impression, what they care about, standing facts, disclosure notes. Dated material belongs in History. A profile that has grown past the budget is split on this pass: move its dated entries to History, keep each fact as one sentence, add the History link at the end of the Profile.
4. Also read the agent's own mailbox for evidence about people. Open specific threads only to verify an identity or a reply; no broad poll, no history backfill. Read each affected profile in full before editing.
5. {human_name}'s own profile, `{human_profile}`, from yesterday's conversations with them. It keeps four sections, under 400 words: **Who** (name, the ids they use on each platform, where they are, timezone), **Working together** (how to address and brief them, hours, channels, what annoys them, and their corrections and directions on your style, format, scope, tone and approach to their work, each dated and merged with its duplicate; under 250 words; it overrides the skills' defaults), **Their people** (links to groups and key profiles) and **On my own** (what you may do without asking), then a link to their portrait. Each `dismissed stop-asking` row in `{agent_dir}/Answered/` since your last run is {human_name} asking for no questions like it: write it as one dated line in **Working together** ("don't ask about <the kind of thing>, dismissed <date>"), merged with a duplicate; other dismissals (`no`, `done`) close one item and teach nothing beyond it.
   **The portrait**, `{human_portrait}`, is your understanding of who {human_name} is, and you keep it. Read `brain portrait` (each line with its id, basis and evidence), `brain goals list --json` and `brain reception summary`, then, from yesterday's conversations and what landed:
   - **New evidence.** Something they said about themselves is a `said` line, in their framing, with today's date; a pattern you have now seen twice is `seen`, stated as behaviour (what they do, decide, ask for, return to, push back on); an inference from one signal is a `guess` with a `test:` (a question to ask once, at a natural moment). Where what they say and what they do differ, keep both, the second as `against:`. Health, mood, relationships and other sensitive subjects appear only as `said`, in their words, never as `seen` or `guess`. Be honest, not flattering.
   - **Infer goals, nightly.** Read `brain goals refused --json` as well as existing goals. Compare yesterday with earlier days and connected evidence you may read: synced Anki hours, calendar, Canvas, Library works, what {human_name} is building or preparing for. Infer what they repeatedly work toward, never from one passing mention or without evidence. Match refused titles, words and aims; never re-propose one. Merge an existing aim by adding evidence. At most two new inferred goals in a night (including earlier seed passes and partial runs), and at most five active inferred goals. Write each through `brain goals new "<title>" --words "<their own words quoted from the source>" --said YYYY-MM-DD --inferred --evidence "<date> · <what> · [[note]]"`, repeatable evidence. Never paraphrase their words, ask whether it is a goal, or wait for confirmation; silence keeps it. Said and inferred goals both fund preparation now, held, with the same high bar for offers and the same outward permissions. The nightly engine sets aside an inferred goal after thirty days without new evidence; edits do not renew it.
   - **After and goals.** Each active goal has one After line naming it (`goal: G-n`), with its `horizon:` and a `for:` naming where you can help. Reached, set-aside, refused or converted goals leave After for History. An unsupported guess funds nothing. A supported aim becomes an inferred goal now; a correction goes to History.
   - **Promote and retire.** A guess whose offers came back `used` or `liked` twice in the reception summary becomes `seen`; one {human_name} confirmed becomes `said`. A guess whose offer was declined, or that they corrected, moves to `History.md` beside the portrait, dated with its id; when they corrected it, their words become a `Not` line, so it is never guessed again. A line contradicted by new evidence is revised, never just appended to; its old version goes to History with the date.
   - Rewrite in place, rank within each section, keep every line's `^id` (write new lines without one, then `brain portrait stamp`), at most eight lines a section and 700 words. When {human_name} answers a line in their app (right, wrong or not this), the engine applies it with `brain portrait mark`; never undo it.
   For a `seen` or `guess` line outside After that is new or changed, add one `confirm` to CURRENT's `## Needs you`, written so {human_name} understands it cold ("I've come to think you … — right?"); count the confirms of this kind in Needs you and History over the last seven days, and add none past two. Their answer is evidence: record it on the line. Your portrait of {human_name} is never told to anyone else.
6. Each profile's title is the person's name once you know it; their ids stay as platform lines (`- **Slack:** \`U…\``, `- **Demiurge:** \`k57…\``). A profile you cannot resolve says so in its title ("unresolved Demiurge speaker"), never matching a rule by accident. The engine logs requests and raises the declines no rule covers; do not add them to Needs you yourself. Keep `{people_dir}/README.md` a current list of profiles, including any Sleep 1 created for someone only mentioned.
7. Overwrite `$AGENT_HOME/records/people/state.json` with the reviewed window and the ids handled, so a rerun does not count a speaker twice. `brain commit -m "people(<date>)" --push`.

## Boundaries
External services read-only; send nothing. No fixed character labels, no sensitive-trait or mental-health inference, no secrets, no raw message dumps. Preserve {human_name}'s edits. Never load `Dreams/`.
