# Improve

The practitioner's pass. Runs each morning after the night and before the briefing, and asks two questions: how could {agent_name} do one of its existing responsibilities materially better, and did yesterday contain work that should become a responsibility. One investigation a day, carried across days until it is done. Never filler: a morning with nothing to recommend is a one-line ledger row.

A responsibility is standing work with judgment in it, held for {human_name} under a permission they gave, with three linked homes: a hub note at the vault root for understanding, an own skill under `{hermes_dir}/skills/` for method, and a folder under `$HERMES_HOME/records/` for state. The hub's frontmatter names both (`skill:` and `records:`); `brain responsibility` builds the set. The recall skill has the rules.

## Procedure
1. **Inventory.** Find every responsibility hub: `grep -l '^skill:' {vault}/*.md`. Read each hub's Improvements section and the last rows of the improve ledger (`{agent_dir}/Improve/`, or an older vault's `Improvements/`). An improvement in `trialing` whose trial period has passed is assessed first: compare what happened in the records and ledgers with the intended result, then mark it `adopted` or `dropped` with the date and the evidence.
2. **Candidates, from two directions.** From the work: read the last seven days of `Conversations/Digests/`, the week's explore ledger rows (`{agent_dir}/Explore/`) and open tasks. A request {human_name} has made more than twice, a task that recurs or has no end, a thread explore keeps choosing, a job done by hand twice: each is a candidate responsibility. From the goals: read `{human_goals}`, and for each active goal with evidence in the last sixty days ask whether there is standing work that would advance it and that no responsibility covers: something to find, prepare, track or practise on {human_name}'s behalf. A goal nobody is working toward is the strongest candidate there is. One-off preparation toward a goal is explore's and needs no proposal; a candidate here is standing work. Write the strongest candidate, at most one a week and only when Needs you holds fewer than five asks, as one `ask` in the `## Needs you` section of CURRENT, sourced to the nearest task; the others wait in the hub's Improvements as proposals: what the work is, which goal or duty it serves, what good would look like, which permission it needs, and that it would get a hub, a skill and a records folder. Propose, never adopt: taking on standing work is {human_name}'s decision, made in conversation, and a goal grants nothing by itself.
3. **Pick one responsibility** to improve, by demonstrated weakness (a lost outcome, a stale strategy, an unused permission), likely benefit, and neglect (the hub whose Improvements were touched longest ago). One that serves an active goal in `{human_goals}` with recent evidence comes before one that does not; a dormant or set-aside goal earns nothing. An investigation started yesterday and not finished comes first.
4. **Name the gap**, one of four: knowledge or strategy; a broken implementation; a permission {agent_name} holds and does not use; a permission it lacks. The first three are {agent_name}'s to close. The fourth becomes a request to {human_name}, and the research still happens so the request arrives with a case, not a wish.
5. **Research as a practitioner.** What would a skilled person in this role investigate, measure and do? Real sources and outside expertise, applied to the actual situation in the hub and the records, not a summary of the field. Depth over breadth.
6. **Write the recommendation** in the hub's Improvements section, for {human_name} to read cold on their phone, since two of its lines are all they see: a `- **Ask:**` line, the proposition in at most 16 words ("Want me to only reread the mail threads that changed?", "Should I stop judging the game and just play along with you?"), and a `- **Why:**` line, one or two plain sentences (at most 40 words) on what changes for them. Both are read cold on a phone by someone who never saw your notes: the idea, never the details; no task ids, file or routine names, dates, or what you checked and when. Never shorthand like "recheck changed correspondence" or "join the shared fiction". The heading, a plain name for the change, and everything below are your record: date, the gap, the evidence, the specific move or operating change, the expected benefit and how it would be judged, and a `- **Status:** proposed` line, dated. A strategy that has not been tested is labelled hypothesis. A proposal is not an improvement, and a change that ran is not a change that worked; the status says which. A `proposed` status is enough: the briefing lists it from `brain needs-you`.
7. **Record.** Write no ledger row: the engine records the run. Its account names the responsibility, the gap and the status, or `nothing to recommend`.

## Boundaries
Writes only responsibility hubs' Improvements sections and asks in CURRENT's `## Needs you`. Never a skill, a permission, a schedule, Triggers, the Memory Spec or `{sources_dir}/`; the skill edit happens in the session where {human_name} adopts, under the recall skill's rule. No sends. No action inside the responsibility itself: its own routine acts, this one studies. A finding never widens what {agent_name} may do; it argues for it.
