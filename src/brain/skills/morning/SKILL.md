# Morning briefing

What today is for {human_name}, at the agreed hour, every day, even when there is little to say. Never before that hour. It reads like a good assistant's word at the door, not a status report: two hundred words is the ceiling, and a hundred is a good morning.

## Procedure
1. Read `{agent_dir}/Primed.md` first; it is the brief the prime routine wrote for today. Then Upcoming, the Calendar block (today and the next seven days) and, when the owner's Canvas is synced, the Canvas block in the session context, Holding, open tasks, any task entries tagged `morning:`, and what `brain needs-you` lists.
2. What is new on the channels reaches the morning through Primed. Then read only the synced mail that arrived since: `brain channels mail --since <last_run in $AGENT_HOME/records/prime/state.json>`, a few headers at most, and add what bears on today under the same rules as prime (people first, a sender {human_name} set aside skipped). Morning does not search mail beyond that. Recheck already-registered threads through their owning tasks before raising an item. Drop an item once they have explained it.
3. Compose in this order, dropping any part with nothing in it, keeping results apart from proposals:
   - **Today.** Lead with the one thing that matters most today, what they must do first. Say where they are only when they are away or travelling, and what is on their calendar when it matters (an event, a stay). Include who they are with, what they said they would do, and what is due or happening today or tomorrow, from Primed, Upcoming and Calendar. One or two sentences, not a list. What {human_name} said they want in Today, or want left out (Working together in their profile), shapes it before anything else. Schoolwork comes from the Canvas block when there is one: what is due today or tomorrow, anything missing, a grade that came in. Only when nothing is due, happening or held does each connected source get a word (the calendar, Primed's channels line, what is due), so a quiet morning is not a blind one; that line never leads. A source that broke since the last briefing gets one line with the fix. With no mail or calendar connected at all, Monday's briefing says so in one line with how to connect. When you can't confirm a due thing was done, say so once: "due today; I can't see that it's done."
   - **Done for you.** What {agent_name} prepared, found or finished since yesterday, and where it is. Every draft held under `{agent_dir}/Prepared/` since the last briefing is named here, at most three, most useful first, one short line each saying what it is and what it is for: this is its one mention, and never again. Check `brain goals list --json` and leave out any draft whose `goal:` names a goal that is not active, even if it is still in Primed; a draft held `for:` their words, a loop or a date stays in. On Demiurge, a held draft that bears on today belongs in `text` below, since nothing else on Today shows it.
   - **Needs you.** The one or two asks that matter most today, a deadline first, one short line each, from `brain needs-you`; the rest wait in the app, and "nothing needs you today" is a good morning. Confirms are not questions: at most one line saying what you went ahead with. Primed's nudges once, only when they bear on today.
   - **Coming up.** The dated items in the next week that need something from them, from Upcoming. Only those; the rest stays in context.
   - **An idea**, only when `brain ideas` shows one serving an active goal (or explicitly asked for): its proposition and the goal it serves in one light line, an offer rather than something they owe. Nothing when it shows none; never more than one.
4. If the night did not run, say so in one sentence, never a list of stages. A responsibility's prepared piece is one line under Done for you, not its contents.
5. After composing, remove every `morning:` line you carried from the task entries with `brain task T-nnn set --clear morning` (never a `## Needs you` item: it leaves when it is resolved): a fact worth keeping goes into the owning note in one sentence, the rest is gone.
6. **Link the calendar to their matters.** Run `brain matters --calendar`: their current matters, and their calendar for the next three weeks. For an event that belongs to a listed matter (the flight of a trip, the move-in day of a move), run `brain matters --soon "<matter's exact title>" --date YYYY-MM-DD --what "<what happens, a few words>"`. A significant event of theirs that no matter holds (travel, a move, a visit, a medical appointment, an exam, an interview) opens one: the same command with a short name of the matter ("Lisbon trip", not the event's title) and `--open` (at most two a day). Never a routine meeting, a class, a recurring event or someone else's event. This is bookkeeping, never part of the briefing.
7. **Deliver it.** On Demiurge the briefing is Today, not a message: call `demiurge_today` with `summary`, one line of at most 240 characters saying what matters about the day (the one thing due first, from the Today part above), and `text`, the one line their phone shows when it should say something the summary does not (Demiurge adds their top asks, numbered, beneath it), or nothing when the day needs nothing from them. Demiurge lists what needs them and what you did beneath it, from the brain, so the summary never repeats those. Only when it returns ok, end the run with exactly `[SILENT]` and nothing else, so the scheduler does not also post your reply to Home. When the tool is not in this session or the call fails, return the briefing itself as your final reply: a scheduled run delivers it to Home. Never end with `[SILENT]` unless Today accepted the briefing; a silent morning is a missed one. A run instruction to return the briefing as your final response and not send it through another tool forbids a duplicate message, not `demiurge_today`. Outside a scheduled run (another platform, a CLI), send the composed briefing to {human_name}'s Home, the destination named in `{agent_dir}/Channels.md`. Either way the engine records the run; write no Sleep Log line.
