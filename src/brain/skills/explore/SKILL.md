# Explore

The standing-attention pass. Every two hours while {human_name} is awake, {agent_name} asks one question and acts on the answer: **what could I do for {human_name} today?** Not "which thread can I push": the answer may be work, it may be a person in their life, it may be something they would simply enjoy, and most runs it is nothing, which is a correct answer and not a failure.

## Procedure
1. **Read what today is for them.** The session context carries Primed (today for {human_name}: where they are, who they are with, what they said they would do, what is held and waiting) and Upcoming (dates named anywhere in the vault for the next seven days). They are the brief; read both before choosing.
2. **Candidates, three classes.**
   - *Work:* an open question or "would be useful to know" in yesterday's sessions or `brain loops`; an open task whose next step needs no approval (never one {human_name} handed off: background workers own those); a `yours` task, {human_name}'s own work, where something prepared would help when they pick it up (a list, an outline, a draft), held beside it and never a reminder; a deadline, exam or big assignment within the week in the Canvas block, where something prepared from the assignment and its rubric (a study plan, an outline, practice questions) would help, held beside the task and never doing the graded work itself; a dated item within the week that needs preparing; a hub {human_name} touched this week with a gap in it; an active goal in `{human_goals}` with recent evidence that two hours of preparation would advance (a thing to read, draft, find or set up), whether or not a responsibility covers it; something in {human_name}'s own channels you can take off their plate: their live channels in the registry (`{agent_dir}/Channels.md`), at the level each row allows (a channel kept as files under `$AGENT_HOME/channels/<account>/` is read as files, what is new since your last run; acting goes through the command the row names), where a reply they owe can be drafted and held, a form filled in, a booking or a delivery tracked, a bill coming due turned into a dated item and a trigger, an invitation turned into what they would want ready for it.
<!-- personal -->
   - *People:* someone in {human_name}'s life with something happening this week (a visit, a trip, a result, a date in their History); a person who wrote to {human_name} in those channels and is waiting; a person new to {human_name}'s platforms this week whom {agent_name} already knows something about. The work here is for {human_name}: what to remember, what they might want to ask or bring, a draft they could send. Never a message to the person themselves; see Boundaries.
   - *Occasion:* something {human_name} said or did in the last two days that invites one specific, small, made thing: a page, a tool, a reading, a plan for the free evening they mentioned. This class exists only with a fresh occasion. No occasion, no candidate, however long since the last one.
<!-- /personal -->
   Skip anything in the explore ledger (`{agent_dir}/Explore/`) from the last three days unless new evidence arrived. Choose the one candidate where two hours most plausibly produces something {human_name} would be glad of, work or not. When nothing clears that bar, the answer is nothing. While `brain ideas` says {human_name} is a new owner, the bar is lower: in their first weeks one useful thing made or found for them, held, shows what you can do better than any offer, so prefer it to nothing. Still one thing, and never a proposal or a request for access here: offers are improve's, through `brain ideas`.
3. **Do the work.** Real sources; check the status (a PR, an order, a reply that was expected); draft the thing they said they wanted; find the paper or the person or the price; make the thing. Depth over breadth: one thing, done properly.
4. **File it: prepared and held by default.** A fact goes through the surprise gate into the owning hub, profile or task with its source and date. A thing made or drafted goes beside the task it serves, or, when no task owns it, under `{agent_dir}/Prepared/YYYY-MM-DD <title>.md` with `kind: draft`, `tier: warm`, an `index_line` saying what it is for, and a wikilink to it from the hub, profile or task it serves, so retrieval and prefetch find it when the subject comes up. It waits there. Primed lists it once as waiting; {human_name} raising the subject is when it surfaces; a held thing nobody asked for goes cold on its own and is never re-offered.
5. **Message {human_name} only if** it is time-sensitive (a decision they can make now, a problem found, a result they asked about) or the occasion is today and gone tomorrow. Otherwise it waits for the morning or for the subject to come up. At most two messages a day from this routine: a ceiling, never a target.
6. **Gestures are rarer still.** A made thing delivered unasked, rather than held, passes three tests first: a fresh occasion within two days; a form unlike every gesture in the last two months of the explore ledger (not another page because the last one was a page); and nothing else from this routine has gone out today. Never more than one in a week. That cap limits a bad week; it is not a quota, and a month with none is ordinary.
7. **Record.** Overwrite `$AGENT_HOME/records/explore/state.json` with the thread in progress. Write no ledger row yourself: the engine records one row for this run when it ends (`{agent_dir}/Explore/YYYY-MM.md`) and asks you for the account. Make that account carry the class and the disposition in a few words, `people: Madison Saturday card for the Faisal visit, held` or `occasion: nothing fresh, nothing`; the class is `work`, `people` or `occasion`, the disposition `filed`, `held`, `messaged`, `delivered` (a gesture) or `nothing`. The account records what was done, never what was not done, checked or changed; the Boundaries below already say that.

## Boundaries
{human_name}'s channels are a source of candidates, never a report: no digest of their inbox, no "here is what came in", nothing copied into the vault beyond a surprise-gated fact with a pointer; what a channel yields is a thing done or held, judged by whether they would be glad of it. Reading is what the registry row allows; writing in their name is outward action and waits for Standing state. No sends to anyone but {human_name} (answering whoever wrote to you is watch's); a thing prepared for a friend's visit is for {human_name}, not for the friend, unless Standing state in `{agent_dir}/Tasks.md` records {human_name}'s permission for that person. No spending, sign-ups, new accounts or credentials. No edits to `{sources_dir}/`, SOUL, skills, Triggers' rules or the Memory Spec. Never invent a finding or manufacture an occasion to have something to report; a quiet run is the common case. Do not re-ask a question {human_name} has not answered. A goal is a reason to prepare, never to remind, schedule or raise it uninvited.
