Every open task now keeps a view, not only those whose result has a shape. A task whose result is a document, an answer, a decision or setup shows its own plan: the stages toward its Delivers or a checklist of what is done, waiting and needed, and links to its work.

1. Run `brain install-skills` to install the current recall and continue instructions, then read the view part of your recall skill.
2. `brain state --json` lists your tasks with any view they keep. For each open task without one (whatever its state: ready, in progress, blocked, waiting, yours or set aside), write one with `brain task T-nnn view` (JSON on stdin, `{"pieces": [...]}`).
3. Fill it only from what the task and its evidence notes record: its Delivers, Next, blockers, ask, checkpoints and work. Use `steps` when it moves through known stages, a `checklist` for what is done, waiting on someone, blocked or needed from your owner, and `links` to its work, draft or source notes. Never invent a stage, a count or a state. A refused view comes back with the kit printed; fix it and write it again.
4. `brain validate`; fix what it names, then `brain migrate-done`.
