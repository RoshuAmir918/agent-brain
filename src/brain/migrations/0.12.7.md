Demiurge identity, and two named destinations: Home and Hearth.

Demiurge sessions carry `platform` demiurge, `user_id` the handle (renamable) and `user_id_alt` the permanent id. The provider now matches a profile line qualified with the session's platform first (``- **Demiurge:** `<permanent id>` ``), before any bare mention, and `owner_user_ids` and `maintainers` accept `demiurge:<id>` entries that match only that platform. Routing has two names: **Home**, where routine output and anything for {human_name} arrives, and **Hearth**, the kingdom's shared room.

Do this without the owner:

1. If `{agent_dir}/Channels.md` has no `## Destinations` section, add one, copying its shape from the starter vault's Channels note in `agent-brain-template`: Home is your Demiurge home conversation with {human_name}, reached by cron with `--deliver demiurge`; Hearth is the kingdom's shared room. Take the Hearth's room id from your maintainer; if you do not have it, leave "the kingdom's shared room".
2. Reword SOUL, the Channels registry and the `## Local` sections of your rendered skills so routing names Home or Hearth, not Slack, channels, threads or ids. Leave history alone: conversations, logs, History.md and dated evidence keep their words.
