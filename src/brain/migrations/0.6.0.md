Process records are now routed away from memory.

Receipts, ledgers, run results and routine self-reports were competing with knowledge for the hot set and the index. The engine now treats any folder listed under `log_dirs` in `brain.toml`, and any note with `kind: log` in its frontmatter, as a record: never activated, never hot, never indexed. Sleep 1, Sleep 2 and Explore now write bounded entries and say what was done rather than what was not.

Do this without the owner, it needs no decisions:

1. List the folders under `{agent_dir}/` you created for receipts, results, sent-mail evidence, check-in payloads or similar, and add them to `brain.toml` as `log_dirs = ["Folder", ...]` (paths relative to `{agent_dir}/`).
2. Any note under `Conversations/` that records one of your own routines rather than an exchange with a person: set its frontmatter to `kind: log` and `tier: cold`.
3. Run `brain validate`, then `brain nightly` without `--write` to confirm nothing you rely on dropped out of the hot set; commit `chore(brain): route process records to log`, then `brain migrate-done`.
