People, explore and watch are core routines now, and maintainers manage engine routines.

`{prefix}people`, `{prefix}explore` and `{prefix}watch` are re-rendered from the engine on every refresh, like the night skills, so improvements reach every agent. A core skill keeps one thing of yours: a `## Local` section at the end of the file, which the render preserves. The refresh that brought this version already overwrote those three files. Also, a maintainer now holds the schedule of the engine's routines (night stages, export, refresh, improve, people, watch, explore, and the template day defaults): create, move, pause, resume, remove or combine on their request, in that session. The hour of the briefing and any routine {human_name} asked for by name stay with {human_name}.

Do this without the owner:

1. For each of the three skills, find your previous version: `git log --oneline -3 -- {hermes_dir}/skills/{prefix}explore/SKILL.md` and `git show <commit before today's render>:<path>`. Compare it with the rendered file.
2. Anything in your version that is still true and not in the rendered procedure (a mailbox to read, an identity check, a roster rule, a state file name, a pitfall you learned) goes under a `## Local` heading appended to the rendered file, as short rules, not a second copy of the procedure. Wording changes to the engine's own steps are dropped; the engine's steps win.
3. `brain install-skills` once more and confirm the Local section survived. `brain validate`.
4. Commit `chore(skills): keep local additions to core routines`, push, then `brain migrate-done`.
