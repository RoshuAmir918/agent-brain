Gists in the tellable note now name no business, venue, person, street, exact time or place smaller than a city; education is filed under work, money under finances, and a past commitment is no longer schedule. Strangers hear gists, so a gist with a venue and a town says where you live.

1. Run `brain install-skills`, then read the seed skill's step 4 for the new rule.
2. Revise `{human_tellable}` by it. Rewrite every gist that names a business, venue, person, street, exact time or a place smaller than a city: an experiences gist keeps the kind of place and how it went, a whereabouts gist the city and roughly when. Move every line about education or a qualification (a degree, course, credential or exam) to `work`, and every line about money (income, savings, spending, prices, budgets, cards or debts) to `finances`, keeping its text, source and gist. Remove every `schedule` line whose date has passed. Merge a moved line into a matching one rather than duplicate it. Change nothing else, and add nothing new.
3. Run `brain validate`, fix what it names, then `brain compile-context --write` and `brain migrate-done`.
