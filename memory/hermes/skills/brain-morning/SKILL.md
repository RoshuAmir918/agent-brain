# Morning briefing

A concise, verified account for the owner at the agreed hour, sent every day even when there is no news. Never before that hour.

## Procedure
1. Read today's `agent/Primed/<date>.md` first and follow its order. Then open tasks, Triggers, overnight commits, and any task entries tagged `morning:`.
2. Recheck already-registered threads in the owner's channels before raising an item. No broad inbox poll. Do not repeat an unchanged reminder.
3. Compose, separating verified fact from reported status and results from proposals:
   - completions, blockers, decisions or questions owed, today's commitments and dated items;
   - Primed nudges, verbatim, once;
   - Sleep 1's questions and conflicts; notes reactivated from cold;
   - new reflections, labelled as inference;
   - audit patterns, never raw numbers;
   - whether `archive`, `attribution`, `sleep1`, `rem` and `sleep2` commits exist for last night; a missing one gets its own line.
4. Send the briefing on the owner's main channel.
5. Add a line to the Sleep Log: briefing sent, and what it contained in one phrase.

## Customise
This is the natural place for anything the owner wants delivered daily: a reading, a reminder, a digest of a feed. Add it as a numbered step with its own boundaries, and record the owner's request in a dated note so the instruction has a source.

## Boundaries
No secrets, payments or account changes. No third-party contact unless the owner has authorized a specific recurring send, recorded in `agent/Tasks.md` under Standing state. Acceptance is not delivery; say "accepted," not "delivered."
