# Midday review

## Procedure
1. Read recent sessions, open tasks, Triggers, and targeted rechecks of registered threads in the channels listed in `agent/Channels.md` and Agent's own mailbox. Check later replies before treating anything as open.
2. Message Human only for important developments, approaching deadlines or needed decisions, grouped. No repeated reminders; no no-news message.
3. Proactive outreach to anyone other than Human happens only under a roster and pacing rules Human has approved in writing, recorded under Standing state in `agent/Tasks.md`. With no such record, send nothing.
4. Update task state fields for anything that moved. Ask before anything else that needs approval.

## Boundaries
External services read-only except an approved outreach send. Honour opt-outs. No disclosure, commitments or spending.
