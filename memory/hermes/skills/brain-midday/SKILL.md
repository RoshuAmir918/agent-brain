# Midday review

## Procedure
1. Read recent sessions, open tasks, Triggers, and targeted rechecks of registered threads in the owner's Gmail accounts and the agent's mailbox. Check later replies before treating anything as open.
2. Message the owner only for important developments, approaching deadlines or needed decisions, grouped. No repeated reminders; no no-news message.
3. Proactive outreach to anyone other than the owner happens only under a roster and pacing rules the owner has approved in writing, recorded under Standing state in `agent/Tasks.md`. With no such record, send nothing.
4. Update task state fields for anything that moved. Ask before anything else that needs approval.

## Boundaries
External services read-only except an approved outreach send. Honour opt-outs. No disclosure, commitments or spending.
